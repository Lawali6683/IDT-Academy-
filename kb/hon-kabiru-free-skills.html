Profile & History

Hon. Kabiru Amadu Mai Palace

A Nigerian politician, entrepreneur, educationist and youth-development advocate. He represents Gusau/Tsafe Federal Constituency of Zamfara State in the House of Representatives, National Assembly, Nigeria.

Early Life and Education

Kabiru Amadu was born and brought up in the Tudun-Wada area of Gusau Local Government Area, Zamfara State, into the humble family of the late Alhaji Mamman Mai Bredi.

  • PrimaryTudun-Wada Model Primary School.
  • SecondaryGovernment Day Junior Secondary School, Samaru, Gusau, then Sambo Secondary School, Gusau, where he served as Head Boy. Senior Secondary School Certificate in 2011.
  • RemedialZamfara College of Arts and Sciences.
  • UniversityB.Sc. Mathematics (Second Class Upper Division), Usmanu Danfodiyo University, Sokoto.
  • PostgraduatePostgraduate Diploma in Education (PGDE), then M.Sc. Mathematics at Ahmadu Bello University, Zaria.

Working Experience

After his NYSC in Zaria, Kaduna State, he returned to Zamfara and established several businesses, creating opportunities for unemployed youths. He also taught and lectured part-time, contributing to educational development and human-capital advancement in his community.

  • Managing Director, Mai Palace Transport
  • Managing Director, Mai Palace Foods Nigeria Limited
  • Chairman, Mai Palace Football Viewing Centre
  • Entrepreneur and business developer
  • Teacher and part-time lecturer

Leadership Positions

  • President, NAMSSN, UDUS Chapter
  • Secretary, Vanguard Press Organisation, UDUS Chapter
  • Pioneer President, UDU Model United Nations Society
  • Liaison Officer, Hope for All Foundation
  • Liaison Officer, TY Danjuma Foundation
  • Communication Officer, Sumayya Support Foundation
  • Chairman, Zamfara Volunteers
  • Chairman, Atiku Care Foundation, Zamfara State
  • Chairman, United Youths for Development, Zamfara State
  • Vice Chairman, BDSS Old Boys Association
  • Secretary, Rebuild Zamfara Initiative
  • National Secretary, Reforming of Nigerian Youths
  • National Secretary, Choose Wisely
  • Organising Secretary, Youth Integrity Forum
  • Organising Secretary, Zamfara State Youth Consultative Forum
  • Adviser, Matasan Zamfara ta Tsakiya

Political Career

His journey moved from student leadership and youth mobilisation to national legislative representation.

  • 2019Elected to represent Gusau/Tsafe Federal Constituency in the House of Representatives, 9th National Assembly.
  • 2023Re-elected for a second tenure in the 10th National Assembly.
  • CommitteeChairman, House Committee on Sports.
  • October 2026Serving his second term and seeking another mandate in the 2027 general elections.

Business and Community

Beyond politics and academia, he has taken part in initiatives aimed at creating employment opportunities, supporting young people and developing communities, through his business ventures and the many organisations he has served.

Personal Life

Kabiru Amadu is married and blessed with children.

From his early beginnings in Tudun-Wada, Gusau, to the National Assembly, his journey reflects education, student leadership, enterprise, community engagement and public service. May Allah continue to guide him and grant the people of Zamfara State wisdom, peace and sustainable development. Amin.

Questions

Simple answers

Read this before you apply.

Is it really free?

Yes. You pay nothing to apply.

Who can apply?

Only indigenes of Gusau or Tsafe. You must upload or take a photo of your indigene letter to prove it.

What do I need?

A phone or computer with a camera, an internet connection, a working phone number and an email address.

Who is behind the programme?

The programme is sponsored by Hon. Kabiru Amadu Mai Palace in partnership with IDT Academy.

What happens after I apply?

Your details are checked and you will be contacted by phone or email. Please keep your phone close and check your spam folder.

Can I apply for another person?

Yes. After you apply, tap Apply for another person. Each person must use their own phone number and indigene letter.
