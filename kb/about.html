Our Story

Why this programme exists

Talent is everywhere. Opportunity should be too.

Many young people in Gusau and Tsafe have talent but no place to learn a skill that can help them. The Hon. Kabiru Amadu Mai Palace Digital Skills Foundations for Youths opens free courses to indigenes of both local governments.

The programme is sponsored by Hon. Kabiru Amadu Mai Palace, who represents Gusau/Tsafe Federal Constituency of Zamfara State, and is delivered in partnership with IDT Academy.

Applying is free, simple and works on a phone, tablet or computer. Every applicant proves they are an indigene of Gusau or Tsafe with an indigene letter, so the programme stays fair for everyone.

Mission & Values

What we believe in

Three ideas guide everything in this programme.

Our mission

To give the youths of Gusau and Tsafe free access to digital and practical skills they can use for work and small business.

Free for you

No payment is needed to apply. Opportunity should never depend on how much money a young person has.

Fair for all

Every applicant shows an indigene letter and uses their own phone number, so places go to real indigenes.
