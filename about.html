Our story

Built carefully, for everyone who wants to learn.

IDT Academy was created to make real education simple for people who want genuine skill and want to use it to reach a goal. It grew out of IDT (Intelligent Digital Technology), a company that builds software, websites, apps, AI and other digital solutions.

The academy was designed and founded by Comrd. Haruna Lawali, CEO of IDT. Ibrahim Abdulhakim, General Manager of IDT, leads quality assurance and security, and guided the testing team to make sure the platform is reliable and comfortable for every learner. Leadership of the academy has been entrusted to Ubaida Aliyu.

After a long period of careful building, the academy officially launched on Monday, 9 November 2026, open to learners everywhere in the world.

“Education should meet you at level zero and walk with you to mastery.”

Trust, security and fair rules

Is my data safe?

Yes. Our servers and learner information are protected carefully, and our platform is built to resist tampering and hacking.

How do you keep assessments fair?

Assessments are timed and monitored, and they use fresh questions so each result reflects what you really know.

Do you accept copied projects?

No. Every project must be original, because your work is promoted publicly under your name.

Can others use our technology?

Not without written permission. All IDT Academy technology and content are protected.

Who can join?

Anyone in the world who wants to learn, from complete beginners to experienced practitioners.
