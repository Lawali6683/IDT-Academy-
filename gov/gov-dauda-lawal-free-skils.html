    Our Sponsor

    Dr. Dauda Lawal

    Born September 2, 1965. A banker, technocrat and politician. Governor of Zamfara State.

    • Born in Gusau, Zamfara State, into the family of Alhaji Lawal Muhammad Dare.
    • B.Sc. Political Science, Ahmadu Bello University, Zaria (1987).
    • M.Sc. Political Science / International Relations, Ahmadu Bello University (1992).
    • Ph.D. Business Administration, Usmanu Danfodiyo University, Sokoto.
    • Executive training at London School of Economics, Harvard Business School, Oxford University Business School and Lagos Business School.
    • 1989: Political education officer with MAMSER. Later worked in the private sector and diplomatic service, including the Nigerian Embassy in Washington, D.C.
    • 2003: Joined First Bank of Nigeria as a relationship manager and rose through many management ranks.
    • 2010: Executive Vice President. 2012: Executive Director, Public Sector North.
    • Co-founded Credent Capital and Advisory Limited.
    • Established the Dr. Dauda Lawal Foundation for community support.
    • Entered active politics before the 2019 elections and contested the 2018 APC governorship primary.
    • Joined the PDP, won its primary and, in March 2023, won the Zamfara State governorship election. He was the first PDP candidate to win it.
    • Took office on May 29, 2023 with a “Rescue Agenda”.
    • Focus areas: security reforms (including the Zamfara Community Protection Guards), healthcare and education (a state of emergency was declared), and urban renewal in Gusau.

    Questions

    Simple answers

    Not sure about something? Tap a question.

    Is it really free?

    Yes. The programme is sponsored by Gov. Dauda Lawal. You do not pay to apply, to learn or to get your certificate.

    Who can apply?

    Any learner can apply. You do not need to have gone to school before. If you can use a phone, you can fill the form.

    What do I need to learn online?

    You need a phone or computer and an internet connection. The lessons are simple and easy to follow.

    What happens after I apply?

    We check your details. When you are approved, we send you an email. Please check your inbox and your spam folder.

    Who is IDT Academy?

    IDT Academy is our training and technology partner. They provide the online lessons, the AI teacher and the certificate.

    Can I apply for another person?

    Yes. After you apply, tap Apply for another person. Each person must use their own email, phone number and ID.
