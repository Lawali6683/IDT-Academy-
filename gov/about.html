About Us

Free skills for every learner in Zamfara

The Zamfara State Free Learning Skill programme is sponsored by Gov. Dauda Lawal. It brings real, practical training to your phone, for free.

Our Purpose

Why this programme exists

We want every young person in Zamfara State to have a real chance to learn, work and build.

Earn

Our Sponsor

Dr. Dauda Lawal

Governor of Zamfara State. A banker, technocrat and politician, born in Gusau on September 2, 1965. He took office on May 29, 2023.

  • B.Sc. and M.Sc. in Political Science from Ahmadu Bello University, Zaria.
  • Ph.D. in Business Administration from Usmanu Danfodiyo University, Sokoto.
  • Executive Director at First Bank of Nigeria, Public Sector North.
  • Focus on security, healthcare and education, including a state of emergency on education.
