How It Works

Know that ignorance is not destiny — learn the modern way at IDT Academy

Whatever course you choose, we make it simple: watch, practice, pass, and get certified. Here is exactly how your learning journey works.

01 Step 1

Learn Anytime, Anywhere

Watch clear videos from expert tutors that show you exactly how things are done — practically, step by step. Learn from home, work, or anywhere, and master real skills at your own speed.

02 Step 2

AI Assignments & Instant Results

After every module there is an assignment. The AI asks the questions and marks you instantly. Score at least 3 out of 5 to move to the next module. The AI explains what you missed before you continue — so you always improve.

When you finish learning, sit the final exam. Pass it, download your result as PDF, and receive your official IDT Academy certificate from the CEO by email — fast and authentic.
