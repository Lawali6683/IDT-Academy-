Minister of State for Defence

Bello Mohammed Matawalle

Born 12 February 1969, Maradun, Zamfara State

He grew up in Maradun, studied, and built a career in public service. He represented the Maru/Maradun Federal Constituency in the House of Representatives, became Governor of Zamfara State in 2019, and in August 2023 President Bola Ahmed Tinubu appointed him Minister of State for Defence.

This foundation carries his home town’s name. Its goal is simple: no young person should be left behind because they could not pay to learn.
