Our story

Bello Mohammed Matawalle was born on 12 February 1969 in Maradun, Zamfara State. He grew up there, studied, and built a career in public service before entering politics.

He represented the Maru/Maradun Federal Constituency in the House of Representatives, became Governor of Zamfara State on 29 May 2019, and completed his tenure on 29 May 2023. In August 2023 President Bola Ahmed Tinubu appointed him Minister of State for Defence.

The foundation carries the name of his home town. Its aim is simple: a young person should never miss a skill only because they cannot pay for it.
