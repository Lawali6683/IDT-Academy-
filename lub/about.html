About Sarauniyar Kasar Hausa

Dr. Lubna Muhammad Baba Gusau is a philanthropist, community leader, education advocate and humanitarian from Zamfara State, Nigeria. Young Foundation Skills brings free online learning to young people, women and every learner who wants to grow.

What the foundation does

Work linked with the Lubna Baba Gusau Foundation (LBG Foundation), based on published reports.

Children's education

Supporting the schooling of children in need.

Health support

Helping patients with hospital bills.

Food and relief

Sharing food and relief items.

Orphans and displaced people

Standing with families who lost their homes.

Water in schools

Providing clean water for pupils.

Women and youth

Empowering girls, women and young people.

Her journey

  1. April 1982Born, according to published biographical reports.
  2. EducationAttended College of Education Staff Primary School, Sokoto, then Usmanu Danfodiyo University, Sokoto. Reports from 2024 say she is studying for a PhD in Criminology and Security Studies at the University of Abuja.
  3. June 2021The foundation announced a water project at Girls Focal Primary School, Gusau.
  4. 2023Reported to be a member of the transition committee linked to the Zamfara State governorship election.
  5. 28 September 2024Installed Sarauniyar Kasar Hausa at a traditional ceremony hosted by the Emir of Daura, Dr. Umar Farouk Umar.
  6. October 2025The LBG Foundation announced her appointment as Africa Director of the Gender Equality Agency under the World Sustainable Development Goals Organization, effective 10 March 2025.

Based on publicly published reports. Some details about her family and full education record need further reliable confirmation.
