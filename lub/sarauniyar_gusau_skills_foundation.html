The history of Dr. Lubna Muhammad Baba Gusau of Zamfara State

Dr. Lubna Muhammad Baba Gusau, widely known as Sarauniyar Kasar Hausa, is a philanthropist, community leader, education advocate and humanitarian in Nigeria. She is from Zamfara State and is linked with work that supports education, empowers women and youth, and helps people in need.

Early life and education

According to published biographical reports, Dr. Lubna was born in April 1982. She attended the College of Education Staff Primary School in Sokoto and then Usmanu Danfodiyo University, Sokoto. Reports published in 2024 say she is studying for a PhD in Criminology and Security Studies at the University of Abuja.

Work and public service

She has worked in education, including as a Senior Assistant Education Officer, and has worked to widen access to learning, especially for girls and young people. She also took part in public service in Zamfara State and was reported to be a member of the transition committee linked to the 2023 governorship election.

Lubna Baba Gusau Foundation

She is linked with the Lubna Baba Gusau Foundation (LBG Foundation). Its work includes supporting the education of children in need, helping patients with hospital bills, sharing food and relief items, supporting orphans and displaced people, providing water in schools, and empowering women and youth. In June 2021 the foundation announced a water project at Girls Focal Primary School, Gusau.

Education and women's empowerment

Her work focuses on supporting children's schooling, opening education to girls, empowering women and helping communities in need. The foundation has also announced plans to support students in girls' secondary schools in Zamfara State.

This history is based on publicly published reports. Some details about her family and full education record need further reliable confirmation.

Sarauniyar Kasar Hausa. Queen of Hausaland.

On 28 September 2024 she was installed Sarauniyar Kasar Hausa at a traditional ceremony hosted by the Emir of Daura, Dr. Umar Farouk Umar, according to published reports. In October 2025 the LBG Foundation announced her appointment as Africa Director of the Gender Equality Agency under the World Sustainable Development Goals Organization, effective 10 March 2025.
