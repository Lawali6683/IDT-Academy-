About Us

Free skills for every Nigerian

The Abdul'aziz Yari Wazobia Foundation Skills programme brings practical, online training to your phone at no cost.

Who we are

A programme built for youth

Wazobia means "come in, everyone". Our doors are open to all.

Our mission

Many young people have talent but no way to learn a skill that pays. We bring free training to your phone so you can learn, practise, and then work, sell or start your own small business. When youth grow, Nigeria grows.

Our vision

A Nigeria where skilled youth build strong families and strong towns, and where no one is left out of learning because of cost or distance.

Patron of the programme
Our Patron

Abdul'aziz Abubakar Yari

A Nigerian leader, businessman and Senator for Zamfara West, born on 28 January 1968 in Talata Mafara, Zamfara State.

  • EducationTalata Mafara Township Primary School and Government Teachers' College, Bakura. Then secretarial studies at Sokoto State Polytechnic and a diploma in Public Administration at Kebbi State Polytechnic.
  • From 1999Joined politics as Zamfara secretary of the APP/ANPP. Later became state chairman and national treasurer of the ANPP.
  • 2007 - 2011Member of the House of Representatives for Anka/Talata Mafara.
  • 2011 - 2019Governor of Zamfara State for two terms. Chairman of the Nigeria Governors' Forum from 2015 to 2019.
  • 2023 - TodaySenator for Zamfara West. Chairman of the Senate Committee on Water Resources.
  • Beyond politicsA businessman active in industry. In December 2025 he received the traditional title Obaloyin of Yorubaland.
